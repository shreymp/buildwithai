# Pitch (one paragraph)

**Tipoff** (working name) is a free, private web app for job seekers who get unsolicited recruiter texts, WhatsApp/Telegram messages, or emails. You paste a message — or the whole conversation — and Tipoff highlights the exact phrases that match known job-scam playbooks (fake-check-and-equipment, "task" scams that make you pay to unlock work, package reshipping, upfront training fees, and identity harvesting), explains each one in plain language, shows where the conversation sits on the scam's step-by-step script, and warns what the scammer will most likely ask for next — before it happens. It never calls a message "safe"; it shows the evidence both ways and what to verify. Everything runs in the browser with transparent rules (no account, no server, nothing uploaded). Problem evidence: the FTC reports job-scam losses rose from $90M (2020) to $501M (2024), and reports of gamified "task" job scams went from ~5,000 in all of 2023 to ~20,000 in the first half of 2024.

# Demo outline (under 3 minutes, recorded)

- 0:00–0:20 Hook: FTC numbers; Maya, a recent grad, gets a text offering $45/hr remote "data entry," interview on Telegram.
- 0:20–1:05 Paste the first message: verdict "Caution"; phrases highlighted ("interview via Telegram", "no experience needed", "$45/hr"); hover shows why; the script timeline shows step 2 of 5 and a "What they'll likely ask next" card: "They'll 'hire' you after a chat-only interview, then mail a check for equipment from their 'approved vendor'."
- 1:05–1:45 Paste the next messages from the real thread: they do exactly that. Verdict flips to "High risk"; timeline advances to step 4; action plan: don't deposit, don't pay the vendor, verify on the company's official careers site, report at ReportFraud.ftc.gov.
- 1:45–2:15 Contrast: a real recruiter email (video interview, company portal, employer-paid background check) → "No known scam playbook matched" plus what still to verify. Shows it isn't paranoid.
- 2:15–2:40 Privacy: turn the network off; it still works. Optional: drop in a screenshot of a text message and it reads it on-device.
- 2:40–2:55 Close: open source, rules are readable, measured accuracy on a held-out test set shown on a "How it works" page.
